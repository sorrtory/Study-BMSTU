[[Формальная грамматика]] которая переписывает один символ.
A   →   α  (один нетерминал слева)

This distinguishes it from a [context-sensitive grammar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Context-sensitive_grammar "Context-sensitive grammar"), which can have production rules in the form α A β → α γ β  with A a nonterminal symbol and α , β  , and γ  strings of terminal and/or nonterminal symbols.
